## 9. What these results do and do not show

They show that, within this prototype's assumptions
([DEDUP_AND_RECOVERY.md](DEDUP_AND_RECOVERY.md#assumptions)), task identity
was preserved and no side effect was performed twice in any tested scenario.
They do not show exactly-once delivery in general, full MCP or A2A
interoperability, or conformance to any IETF document
([LIMITATIONS.md](LIMITATIONS.md)).
